Covering release unit that makes all four mandatory quality gates — `go test ./...`, `go vet ./...`, `golangci-lint run`, and `gofmt -l .` — green by fixing the shared line-ending root cause (`.gitattributes text=auto` producing CRLF on Windows checkout) and the full enumerated golangci-lint debt across the supported-platform surfaces, and installs a persistent CI line-ending guard so line endings cannot silently re-drift.

## Baseline

The authoritative baseline is the complete unbounded golangci-lint v2.13.2 structured run across the supported-platform surfaces `windows` and `linux`. The Windows-primary surface carries exactly 489 findings across 160 files (459 errcheck + 30 staticcheck); the Linux surface adds 8 Linux-only errcheck findings on `_unix.go` sources and excludes 4 Windows-only findings on `_windows.go` sources. The unique supported-platform union is exactly 497 findings (intersection 485). It is captured with the same invocation semantics as `scripts/verify-baseline-lint.ps1` (`--max-issues-per-linter 0 --max-same-issues 0 --uniq-by-line=false`), recorded in `docs/decisions/2026-09-17-baseline-lint-inventory.md`, and bound to a committed machine-readable inventory at `docs/decisions/baseline-lint-inventory.json` (SHA-256 of the canonical checked-in LF inventory bytes — equivalently the post-U1 LF working-tree bytes, not the host CRLF checkout — `bbb20803209fcf26af03e8dd13af1ad6236629fb2c7432caf14be5badddc5f4c`). The inventory declares `primary_goos: windows`, `supported_goos: [windows, linux]`, the 489-row Windows `findings` array, the 8-row Linux-only `additional_findings` array (each tagged `goos: linux`), and `surface_exclusions` (`windows`: 0, `linux`: the 4 Windows-only keys). Durable ownership identity is the line-stable group-multiset model (`identity_model: line-stable-group-multiset/v1`, schema `baseline-lint-platform-inventory/v3`): the stable group key is the line-independent tuple `(path, linter, normalized_message)` fingerprinted as `sha256(json([path, linter, normalized_message]))`, and each row carries `group_fingerprint`, `group_ordinal`, and `group_multiplicity`. `line` and `column` are retained only as diagnostic baseline coordinates and are never part of ownership identity. Every governed occurrence in the union is owned by exactly one remediation task as an exact disjoint occurrence-count (multiplicity) partition of its stable group; the 6 same-file split groups are owned by ordered slice chains over contiguous ordinal ranges, and each slice records its `expected_group_residual_after_task`. The inventory's `group_index` records, per stable group, the multiplicity and the owning task(s) with their `owned_count`, `owned_ordinals`, and expected residual.

## Release-unit contract

This release unit executes under the installed fail-closed `baseline-convergence` contract: task-scoped lint per member at intermediate waves through the canonical task runner, exact remaining-baseline monotonicity verified at each intermediate wave, and mandatory zero-warning global lint only at terminal convergence through the canonical terminal runner (`scripts/verify-terminal-lint.ps1`) covering both declared supported surfaces (`windows`, `linux`). Same-wave sibling harnesses may remain red until wave convergence; no later-wave harness or lint scaffolding is created early.

The three shared runners this contract invokes — `scripts/verify-task-lint.ps1` (per-task), `scripts/verify-baseline-lint.ps1` (intermediate wave), and `scripts/verify-terminal-lint.ps1` (terminal) — do NOT exist on this branch; they are OWNED and created by the runner-bootstrap prerequisite member `175.099-T`, shipped in the prerequisite shipment `176-S` (RS-W(-1)) that gates the replacement baseline sequence (`176-S -> 157-S`). ## Terminal convergence (U12)

U12 runs the build/vet/format gates (`go test ./...`, `go vet ./...`, `gofmt -l .`) and delegates the mandatory zero-warning terminal lint gate to the canonical terminal runner (`scripts/verify-terminal-lint.ps1 -FeatureId 175-F`), which screens both supported GOOS surfaces (`windows`, `linux`) via an in-process `GOOS` override on a single host and emits `TERMINAL-LINT-OK:175-F` only after both surfaces prove zero residual warnings; U12 fails on any nonzero exit, a missing `TERMINAL-LINT-OK:175-F` marker, or non-empty gofmt output, and validates its `docs/closure/` evidence artifact: gate markers as exact unique lines and a machine-readable full-identity `//nolint` suppression inventory rejecting missing, extra, duplicate, empty-justification, and stale rows and delimiter tampering, using lexical-state-aware scanning, a continuous NUL-delimited byte-stream tracked-file enumeration, strict throw-on-invalid UTF-8 path decoding, ordinal-identity comparison, capitalization-drift detection, and U+00AD soft-hyphen rejection. It requires valid Docline closure frontmatter and passing docs lint. Content-binding of the evidence and the governed-claim union is enforced by the canonical runner's `-Phase PreCommit` (evidence records the current `HEAD`; the only working-tree/index delta is the evidence artifact) and `-Phase PostCommit` (evidence records `HEAD^`; the current commit changes exactly the evidence artifact) lifecycle screens over the closed governed-claim allowlist (`.backlogit/queue/175.012-T.md` and `.backlogit/hooks_queue.jsonl`); both modes fail closed on any ambiguous mode or any dirty source, config, test, script, workflow, or extra path. U12 owns only its named evidence artifact under `docs/closure/` and carries a canonical `no-go-lint-surface` task-lint contract proving its owned delta carries no lintable Go surface.

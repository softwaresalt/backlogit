Reload/CAS under lock (reconcile stale-snapshot clobber protection). AssociateCommit READS the full artifact BEFORE acquiring its locks (commits.go:60-102), so even with a coherent order its STALE pre-lock snapshot can OVERWRITE a completed reconciliation after locks are released (it writes archived_status back from the stale copy). Require AssociateCommit (and any snapshot-before-lock artifact writer that can touch a shipment frontmatter) to RE-READ/RE-VALIDATE the artifact AFTER acquiring its locks (read-under-lock or compare-and-swap on the on-disk state) so a stale snapshot cannot clobber reconcile archived_status:shipped write. This is a CLOBBER-safety requirement, independent of deadlock (A and B are bounded-wait; see 167.008). TDD: a test where a stale-read writer is prevented from clobbering a concurrent reconcile write. Single domain (reload/CAS). Domain: code (concurrency). Deps 167.017. Blocks 167.020.

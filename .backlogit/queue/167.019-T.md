DEADLOCK PREREQUISITE part 3/4 (reload/CAS under lock) - split from 763A1152. AssociateCommit READS the full artifact BEFORE acquiring its locks (commits.go:60-102), so even with a coherent order its STALE pre-lock snapshot can OVERWRITE a completed reconciliation after locks are released. Require AssociateCommit (and any snapshot-before-lock artifact writer) to RE-READ/RE-VALIDATE the artifact AFTER acquiring its locks (read-under-lock or compare-and-swap on the on-disk state) so a stale snapshot cannot clobber a concurrent write. TDD: a test where a stale-read writer is prevented from clobbering a concurrent write. Single domain (reload/CAS). Domain: code (concurrency). Deps 167.018. Blocks 167.020.

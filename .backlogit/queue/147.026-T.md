**Cycle-17 narrowed evidence-pair claim.** The payload move uses `moveNoReplace` and is genuinely no-clobber; the **sidecar** is written with `atomicfile.WriteFileAtomic`, documented as an idempotent upsert, and therefore **replaces** an existing destination. The pair is protected in aggregate only because the payload move runs first and refuses the collision before the sidecar step is reached. Assert exactly that - payload no-clobber and the pair intact after a refused second quarantine - and make **no** claim that the sidecar write is itself no-replace. Cycles 16 and earlier stated the stronger claim; it is false against the shipped code. Sidecar hardening is follow-up stash `A12BBAFA`.

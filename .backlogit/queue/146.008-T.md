Plan unit U3b. Guard the observable behavior of ParseCheckpoint for every context shape that now flows through a hand-written UnmarshalJSON. The expected outcomes MUST be captured from the PRE-U2 commit as a committed golden table and generated once before U2 begins; writing expectations after U2 is designed would encode post-change behavior as today and make the guard pass vacuously. Scenario 2 is widened by the PR #372 remediation cycle: the duplicate-key table MUST include both the exact-duplicate case and BOTH mixed-case alias orders - {"shipment_id":"A","shipment_id":"B"}, {"shipment_id":"A","Shipment_ID":"B"}, and {"Shipment_ID":"A","shipment_id":"B"} - because encoding/json resolves aliases in SOURCE order. Pinning both orders is what makes the trap detectable: a source-order-faithful decoder satisfies both rows deterministically while a decoder that resolves modeled aliases by iterating map[string]json.RawMessage can satisfy them only by chance and will flake. No row may be recorded as "expected: either value".

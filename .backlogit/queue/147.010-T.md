Plan unit **U5b**. Pin the pre-existing state-conflict class that invariant I3 is scoped to exclude.

Files: `internal/core/checkpoint_disposition_test.go`. **No production change.**

A conforming, valid, `status: "resolved"` checkpoint is refused by `abandon` with `ErrCheckpointNotActive` **and** by `quarantine` with `ErrCheckpointUseAbandon`. This is shipped behaviour, not introduced by this feature, and widening quarantine to accept it is out of scope — it would change what "quarantine" means, from "these bytes are untrustworthy" to "I want this file gone".

Leaving it untested would let a future reader believe I3's totality claim covers it. I3 is total only over `status: "active"`.

**Posture: regression guard — green on landing, no red phase.**

- A `status:"resolved"` conforming row asserts both refusals with their exact sentinels
- A `status:"active"` conforming row asserts abandon accepts, proving the discriminator is `status` and not conformance
- The exclusion is tested rather than assumed

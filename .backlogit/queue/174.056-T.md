Behavior RED **regression** harness for the flat-manifest edge cases (spec §0.5 / SBLK-R28; plan §0.4), kept tightly coupled to flat-manifest semantics. Compiles against current code and is RED until SCOPE-IMPL-2 (`174.058-T`) lands. Asserts BEHAVIOR only; no production change.

Scenarios (<4, exactly 3), each anchored to a surface `174.058-T` owns (member projection + ship/closure feature cleanup):
1. **Unlisted blocked descendant excluded from the projection + not orphaned on ship** — a descendant of a listed feature whose status is `blocked` and whose ID is NOT in the manifest is absent from `size_composition.members`, and shipping the manifest does NOT return-to-backlog / `clearParentID`-orphan it (`returnUnreleasedFeatureItems`). Membership is by explicit listing, not by status or hierarchy.
2. **Archived descendants excluded from the projection + not re-touched on ship** — descendants of a listed feature that are `archived` (the `174.001-T…174.038-T` band archetype) are absent from `size_composition.members` and are NOT collected by `collectArchiveCandidateIDs` on ship; archival is NOT the descoping mechanism — non-membership is. No archived/superseded task is restored to satisfy scope.
3. **Feature-only / zero-executable-task manifest** — a manifest containing only a covering feature (no listed task members) scopes to `{feature}`; ship/closure completes only listed members, completes/archives NO unlisted descendant, and **frees the single active slot** (P-001) rather than stranding it. This is the active-slot-safety case for zero executable tasks.

AC: (1) unlisted blocked descendant absent from `size_composition.members` and not orphaned on ship; (2) archived descendants absent from the projection and not re-collected by archival cleanup (archival not required to descope); (3) feature-only manifest → scope == `{feature}` and the active slot is freed on ship/closure with no unlisted descendant completed/archived; (4) harness RED before `174.058-T`, GREEN after. Domain: tests. <4 scenarios.

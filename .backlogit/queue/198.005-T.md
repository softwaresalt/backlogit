entries active. CT does not widen to fix it.

gated delta). On a non-green branch (a second failure of the same package, a
package timeout, a first run failing in two or more packages, or a failure in a

and does not loop or mark the task BLOCKED, and Ship reports the failure through
P-005 and halts with 198-S left active (no `backlogit_return_blocked` call; plan
A1.5), and Stage captures the policy's stash entry when it triages the halt. Run
with no other `go test` run, build, or heavy host activity, and save the
command's complete output (no `-v`, no summary or tail) to the evidence log path.

## Objective

Optimize `events.ReadAllEvents` scanner buffer allocation in `internal/events/reader.go` (`76553D8D` / `097-DL` / Unit U2) by starting with a 64 KiB initial buffer that grows on demand up to 1 MiB max instead of eagerly allocating and zeroing 1 MiB on every call.

## Scope and Affected Files

* **Domain**: `code` (`internal/events`)
* **Execution posture**: `test-first`
* **Affected files (2)**:
  * `internal/events/reader.go`
  * `internal/events/reader_test.go`

## Implementation Details

1. In `internal/events/reader.go` (`ReadAllEvents`, line 40), change `scanner.Buffer(make([]byte, 1<<20), 1<<20)` to `scanner.Buffer(make([]byte, 64*1024), 1<<20)` (64 KiB initial buffer, growing on demand up to the 1 MiB maximum token size, preserving `TestU175_028_LintFix` close-error handling).

## Acceptance Criteria

* **AC1 (RED-First Harness)**: `TestU2_ReadAllEventsSmallInitialBufferGrowsToOneMiBMax` in `internal/events/reader_test.go` compiles cleanly before `reader.go` is edited and fails on a deterministic runtime assertion (`assertion RED`): (a) after a warmup call and `runtime.GC()`, the minimum deterministic heap allocation (`runtime.ReadMemStats` `TotalAlloc` delta across 3 runs) for `ReadAllEvents` on a small 2-line event log is `< 256 * 1024` bytes (failing deterministically against the current `1,048,576`-byte eager allocation); (b) an event line larger than 64 KiB (e.g., 256 KiB payload) grows the scanner buffer on demand and parses cleanly; and (c) an oversized line (`1<<20 + 1` bytes) still fails closed with `bufio.ErrTooLong`.
* **AC2 (GREEN Verification)**: `go test -timeout=30m -count=1 -race -v -run '^(TestU2_ReadAllEvents|TestReadAllEvents|TestTailEvents|TestU175_028_LintFix)' ./internal/events` passes with 0 failures.

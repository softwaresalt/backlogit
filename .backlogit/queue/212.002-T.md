Lockfile-provenance binding contract: (a) a `workspace-local-pinned` `ToolEntry` must declare at least one of a non-empty `pinned_version` (`strings.TrimSpace(tool.PinnedVersion) != ""`) or a non-empty `lockfile_path` (`strings.TrimSpace(tool.LockfilePath) != ""`); if both are empty, `VerifyWorkspaceToolchain` rejects the entry (`missing version or lockfile pin`); (b) whenever `strings.TrimSpace(tool.LockfilePath) != ""` is specified (whether alone as lockfile-only provenance or alongside `pinned_version`), `verifyLocalTool` opens `lockfile_path` via the pinned workspace `*os.Root` handle (`openLocal`), reads its UTF-8 content, and verifies: (1) the lockfile is non-empty (`len(bytes.TrimSpace(data)) > 0`), (2) the lockfile content contains the tool's trimmed `ID` (`strings.Contains(string(data), strings.TrimSpace(tool.ID))`), and (3) when `strings.TrimSpace(tool.PinnedVersion) != ""` is also set, the lockfile content also contains the trimmed `PinnedVersion` (`strings.Contains(string(data), strings.TrimSpace(tool.PinnedVersion))`).

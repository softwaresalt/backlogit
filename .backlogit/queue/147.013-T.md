Its `ErrCheckpointNotFound` -> `NotFound` case and its `default: InternalError` tail both stay.
2. Extend `checkpointDispositionErrorResponse` with `UnknownFields []string` (`unknown_fields`), `UnknownFieldsTruncated bool`, `UnknownFieldsOmitted int`, and `UnknownFieldsShortened int` - **none with `omitempty`** - populated via `errors.As` from 147.030-T / U1b''s `BoundedFieldPaths()`. **Cycle-17: `unknown_fields` carries RAW paths.** No `strconv.Quote` output and no synthetic `+N more` element ever appears in the array; truncation is reported by the three sibling scalars. Earlier text calling those two rows "moved to U7e" was stale and is removed - they were deleted, not relocated.
4. Derive the operator-facing verb from the first word of `op` and interpolate `backlogit_<verb>_checkpoint` into the "instead of" clause of the three disposition-class remediation strings, so a resolve refusal does not advertise `backlogit_abandon_checkpoint` as the operator''s original verb after 147.025-T / U7d routes it through the same formatter.

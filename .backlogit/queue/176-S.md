## Prerequisite shipment (RS-W(-1)) — gates the entire replacement baseline sequence

`176-S` carries the single runner-bootstrap prerequisite task `175.099-T`, which
OWNS creation of the three shared lint runners
(`scripts/verify-task-lint.ps1`, `scripts/verify-baseline-lint.ps1`,
`scripts/verify-terminal-lint.ps1`) that every baseline-remediation member's
`task_lint_cmd` depends on.

This shipment is the FRONT of the replacement chain: `157-S depends_on 176-S`.
The wave-aligned replacement baseline sequence `157-S`..`169-S` (RS-W00..RS-W12)
MUST NOT begin until `176-S` has shipped, because before the shared runners exist
no member has an executable task-lint command. The task-only manifest (no `175-F`
member) matches the descendant-expansion avoidance used by `157-S`..`169-S`.

`175.099-T` carries a SELF-CONTAINED bootstrap harness/lint contract that proves
the three created runners exist, are tracked, parse cleanly under the PowerShell
AST parser, and declare their required parameter surface — using only existing
main-branch tooling (`go test` + `pwsh`) and NEVER invoking the runner it creates
(a non-vacuous bootstrap; see `.backlogit/queue/175.099-T.md` and
`docs/decisions/2026-09-19-baseline-convergence-decomposition.md`).

No Go source, workflow, or runtime implementation ships here; the deliverable is
the three imperative PowerShell runners and one Go harness, produced by Ship
during execution of `175.099-T`.

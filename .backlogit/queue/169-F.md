Adds opt-in verification capability for #423 residual (1) cryptographic authenticity
(866FDC8C); full closure requires enforcement mode (see plan; deferred in B6). Verifies a
CI/release-system SIGNED attestation over the reconcile delivery evidence
(shipment-id + merge-sha + manifest-digest + closure-content-hash) against a
Feature-A (168-F) trusted verification key, and binds the verified attestation
IDENTITY into the `shipment_reconciled_shipped` event so `doctor` can distinguish
a signed repair path from an unsigned one. NOTE: `doctor` distinguishes by the
presence of attested metadata fields, not by re-verifying a retained signature;
independent verification from the event record requires the original attestation
artifact. Opt-in --attestation path on reconcile-shipped; unconfigured = #423 v1
behavior. Depends on 168-F and on the shipped #423 surface (167-F/148-S). Plan
docs/exec-plans/2026-09-06-866fdc8c-trust-boundary-split-plan.md.

# Zero-Flake Concurrency and Fast Ship-Closure Engine (CAP-3)

## Summary

Covering feature for **CAP-3 (Zero-Flake Concurrency & Fast Ship-Closure Engine)**, delivering six coupled concurrency, lock-ordering, subprocess-timeout, and per-member lookup performance fixes across `internal/core`, `internal/db`, and `internal/events`:

* **`703835DC` (`096-DL`, Unit U1 — `216.001-T`)**: Enforce canonical lock ordering `lifecycle-global -> C (item-log) -> B (artifact mutation)` in `LinkCommit` and `AssociateCommit` (`internal/core/commits.go`) across persistence and compensation, failing closed with `*ShipmentLifecycleLockOrderError` on preheld `C` without verified `lifecycle-global` ownership, with forced-interleaving shipment-member regression tests in `internal/core/reconstitution_review_regression_test.go`.
* **`76553D8D` (`097-DL`, Units U2, U3a, U3b — `216.002-T`, `216.003-T`, `216.004-T`)**: Optimize `ShipShipment` per-member snapshot, lookup, ancestry, and scanner-buffer cost while preserving both gate passes, verdict timing, error classification, and lock order:
  * Reduce `events.ReadAllEvents` (`internal/events/reader.go`) initial scanner buffer from eager 1 MiB to 64 KiB growing up to 1 MiB max (`216.002-T`).
  * Add `findArtifactWithPath` with a two-pass search across `artifactSearchDirs(ws)` (Pass 1: direct `{id}.md` probe with `ensureArtifactLookupContained` + `parseFile`; Pass 2: `filepath.WalkDir` fallback) in `internal/core/artifacts.go` and use it in `snapshotShipArtifacts` (`internal/core/shipment_lifecycle.go`) to eliminate duplicate per-member directory walks (`216.003-T`).
  * Pass `--no-replace-objects` to `git merge-base --is-ancestor` and memoize verified 40/64-hex `(ancestorSHA, descendantSHA)` commit ancestry results in a bounded `Workspace`-scoped cache in `isAncestor` (`internal/core/shipment_gate.go`, `internal/core/workspace.go`) restricted strictly to complete, ungrafted history (`.git/shallow` and `.git/info/grafts` absent, invalidating/bypassing on graph boundary changes) across members and across the two gate passes (`216.004-T`).
* **`F448AA52` (`098-DL`, Units U4 & U6 — `216.005-T`, `216.007-T`)**: Place `_pragma=busy_timeout(30000)` first in `db.Open` (`internal/db/connection.go`) and separately materialize the persistent Page 1 WAL header (`hdr[18]==2, hdr[19]==2`) on empty `.db` files and verify WAL-mode / `commit_links` schema initialization across pooled connections (`internal/db/connection.go`, `internal/db/schema.go`) with a concrete concurrent pooled-connection reproducer (`216.005-T`); seed `newAuthSeamFixture` via `sync.Once` (`internal/core/shipment_reconcile_auth_seam_test.go`) and bound child subprocess execution/readiness in `TestUR1B_FailClosedValidationAndAuthoritativeActiveSlot` (`internal/core/shipment_blocked_lifecycle_harness_test.go`) (`216.007-T`).
* **`9CFC88E5` & `B25BF131` (`099-DL`, `100-DL`, Unit U5 — `216.006-T`)**: Bound `custodyGit` (`internal/core/custody_exec.go`) with a self-derived 15s timeout, `cmd.WaitDelay = 2 * time.Second`, `-c core.fsmonitor=false -c gc.auto=0`, and `GIT_TERMINAL_PROMPT=0`, deduplicate `custodyBlobDigest` across identical immutable Git blob OIDs, and harden `buildCustExecSeed` and `TestU4_CustodyPreviewOfferAcceptVerifyAndRecovery/profile_b_stage_to_ship_handoff_and_stale_digest_invalidation` (`internal/core/custody_exec_test.go`) under concurrent `-race` load while retaining `PRAGMA synchronous = FULL` in `custExecNewRepo`.
* **`63909DAE` (`101-DL`, Unit U7 — `216.008-T`)**: Emit deterministic `"attempt_membership"` and `"attempt_item_log"` hook stages in `lockArchiveGovernance` (`internal/core/archive.go`) and synchronize `TestU172_LockOrder_ArchiveItemDoesNotHoldArtifactLockWhileWaitingForItemLog` (`internal/core/artifact_mutation_lock_order_test.go`) and `TestArchiveItemGovernance_WaitsForGlobalLifecycleLock` (`internal/core/shipment_archive_governance_test.go`) on deterministic contention signals with 30s post-unlock completion budgets.

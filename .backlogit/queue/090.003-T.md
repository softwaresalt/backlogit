Stop running golangci-lint once per Go matrix cell. Evaluate whether CI lint should remain as a single de-duplicated CI gate or be replaced by a reliable local enforcement path.

<!-- BEGIN:acceptance-criteria -->
* golangci-lint runs at most once per CI workflow run, not once per matrix cell.
* Either a single de-duplicated CI lint job or step remains, or CI lint is removed only after adding and documenting a reliable local lint gate such as a pre-commit hook and/or make lint/dev-build flow.
* The chosen lint path preserves Constitution I by keeping golangci-lint pass/fail enforceable and visible to maintainers.
* No branch-protection required status check is silently removed or left pending if lint job names are changed.
<!-- END:acceptance-criteria -->

<!-- BEGIN:implementation-notes -->
Preferred low-risk path is a single CI lint gate. Removing CI lint is acceptable only with a concrete local replacement and operator-visible documentation. Coordination note: required-check rules may need an update if lint becomes its own named check.
<!-- END:implementation-notes -->

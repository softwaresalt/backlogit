Size, size_source, and size_ruleset_version are optional backlogit-owned extensions layered on the docline base Markdown/frontmatter contract; docline does not calculate, default, aggregate, validate domain semantics, or emit size. Bounded investigation for PR #241 found the current core.SetArtifactSize seam accepts only (id, size) with no provenance inputs and emits no event, item history is append-only JSONL (best-effort, warn-on-failure), and no canonical XS-XL aggregation ruleset exists. Because provenance/history atomicity and ruleset ownership remain unresolved, this feature is refocused as a time-boxed contract/architecture spike (108.001-T) whose proceed outcome gates the blocked implementation follow-ups (108.002-T/108.003-T/108.004-T).

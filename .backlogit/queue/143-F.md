Guarantee the shipment shipped-event audit log is durable across the governed active -> shipped -> archived transition driven by ShipShipment, so a shipment shipped through the governed ShipShipment path can never persist archived_status: shipped while the append-only JSONL omits shipment_status_changed: shipped (or an explicit indeterminate reconciliation error is surfaced instead). Generic non-ShipShipment transition/archive prevention that would close bypass paths such as UpdateArtifactWithGate remains deferred to stash 47B48DB0; the report-only doctor audit added here is the standing detection net for historical or bypass-produced residue. Integrates an error-returning shipped-event append into the hardened ShipShipment rollback envelope with class-aware, two-class durable-write handling (NotApplied compensates; Indeterminate/untagged never rolls back and surfaces a MutationPartialError), scoped to the shipped transition, and adds a report-only doctor audit (detection net) plus its CLI and MCP surfaces. Completes 140-F Unit 1 (deferred from 106.033-T). Source: stash 0115F71F; deliberation 059-DL.

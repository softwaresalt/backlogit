## Description

<!-- BEGIN:description -->
Guarantee the shipment shipped-event audit log is durable across the governed active -> shipped -> archived transition driven by ShipShipment, so a shipment shipped through the governed ShipShipment path can never persist archived_status: shipped while the append-only JSONL omits shipment_status_changed: shipped (or an explicit indeterminate reconciliation error is surfaced instead). Current source already routes the active-to-shipped shipment_status_changed emission through an error-returning per-ws append seam (ws.shipmentEventAppend) and propagates append failure via shipmentEventAppendError from moveShipmentStatusWithHeadGuard; that seam is the characterized baseline, not new work in this feature. The remaining planned work delivered here, test-first, is the failure-taxonomy and rollback behavior around that seam -- class-aware NotApplied vs Indeterminate handling of the captured shipped-append error, untagged-error safe-default plus clean pre-append lock-acquisition NotApplied tagging, synchronous under-lock covering-feature rollup restoration for indeterminate outcomes with a MutationPartialError return, and event-ordering/compensation coverage -- plus a report-only doctor audit (detection net) and its CLI and MCP surfaces with CLI/MCP parity. Generic non-ShipShipment transition/archive prevention that would close bypass paths such as UpdateArtifactWithGate remains deferred to stash 47B48DB0; the report-only doctor audit added here is the standing detection net for historical or bypass-produced residue. Completes 140-F Unit 1 (deferred from 106.033-T). Source: stash 0115F71F; deliberation 059-DL.
<!-- END:description -->

## Goals

<!-- BEGIN:goals -->
- Make the shipment_status_changed: shipped event durable before archival can proceed; archival must not continue without it.
- On append failure: compensate active/unarchived state (NotApplied) or surface an explicit indeterminate reconciliation error (Indeterminate), never a silent success.
- Behave identically across the shared CLI and MCP core path.
- Provide a report-only doctor audit detecting archived shipments with archived_status: shipped but no shipped event, and shipped-but-unarchived residue; never rewrite historical JSONL.
<!-- END:goals -->

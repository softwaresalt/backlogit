# Shipment shipped-event audit-log durability and doctor reconciliation

## Description

<!-- BEGIN:description -->
Guarantee the shipment shipped-event audit log is durable across active -> shipped -> archived transitions, so a shipment can never persist archived_status: shipped while the append-only JSONL omits shipment_status_changed: shipped. Verified against clean origin/main 3ec95ee3: the shipped path still emits through the best-effort appendItemEvent (internal/core/shipment.go:205) after the status is already persisted (:200), and ShipShipment archives unconditionally (internal/core/shipment_lifecycle.go:515-527); no shipment-scoped append seam and no shipment append-error classification exist. This feature introduces a per-workspace shipment append seam plus a shipment-scoped error-returning appender, routes ONLY the active -> shipped transition fail-closed, classifies the captured append error per the two-class durable-write contract (ErrWriteNotApplied and untagged compensate; tagged ErrWriteIndeterminate never rolls back and surfaces a MutationPartialError), makes compensation honest rather than silently partial, corrects the adjacent defer ordering so the non-member covering-feature fallback runs with locks held, and adds a report-only doctor reconciliation audit emitting two distinct findings plus its CLI, MCP, recovery-guidance, and policy surfaces. Prevention of non-ShipShipment producers is deferred to active stash 47B48DB0. Source: stash 0115F71F; deliberation 059-DL.
<!-- END:description -->

## Goals

<!-- BEGIN:goals -->
- No shipment can reach archived_status: shipped without a durable shipment_status_changed: shipped event.
- An append failure is classified honestly: not-applied compensates, tagged indeterminate never rolls back and returns a structured MutationPartialError.
- Compensation is all-or-nothing or reports itself as partially-compensated with the un-restored IDs.
- Historical residue is detectable through a report-only audit that never writes or rewrites JSONL.
- The audit is reachable from both the CLI and MCP, and the MCP recovery guidance names a check that can actually detect the residue.
- Policy, the reconcile skill, and the Ship agent no longer instruct git restore over the intended residue.
<!-- END:goals -->

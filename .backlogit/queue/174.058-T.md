Update the tightly-coupled existing tests that encode the old expand-and-cleanup behavior (e.g. `TestShipShipment_CleansReleasedFeatureScope` in `shipment_test.go`, the archive-cascade assertions in `archive_test.go`).

Kept tightly coupled to flat-manifest semantics.

title: 'newRenderer: accept format.Format instead of raw string'
updated_at: 2026-04-20T13:55:49.4269387-07:00
---

Advisory finding from 035-S review (F-003, P3): `newRenderer` in `internal/cli/list.go` accepts a raw `string` parameter, internally casts to `format.Format`, and silently defaults to table output for unknown values. This weakens the typed format API.

Recommended fix: Change signature to `newRenderer(f format.Format, w io.Writer) format.Renderer` and use an explicit switch or return `(format.Renderer, error)` for unknown formats.

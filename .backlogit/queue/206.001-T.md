On `ErrWriteIndeterminate` during `RemoveFromShipment` (whether on the member write, shipment manifest write, or audit append), `RemoveFromShipment` skips in-memory compensation and retains the `remove_item` journal on disk for `lockShipmentLifecycleGlobal` barrier recovery; on `ErrWriteNotApplied`, `RemoveFromShipment` rolls back in-memory from `Preimage.Shipment` and `Preimage.Members` (restoring both the shipment manifest and `scheduler_baseline_claim`) and removes the journal (covered in `TestU1_RemoveFromShipment_QueuedAndForceGuards` by claimed-member forced-removal, crash-between-writes `remove_item` journal recovery through `lockShipmentLifecycleGlobal`, `ErrWriteIndeterminate` journal-retention recovery, and `ErrWriteNotApplied` rollback tests).
* `blocked` shipments (`status == "blocked"` or carrying a blocked-manifest seal) are unconditionally refused (`ErrShipmentBlockedManifestSealed` / `ErrInvalidStatus` / `errors.Is(err, blerrors.ErrShipmentConflict)`) even when `Force: true` — in both live (`DryRun: false`) and preview (`DryRun: true`) modes — requiring governed `UnblockShipment` first; `Force: true` with non-empty `Reason` overrides only `active` (and terminal `shipped`/`abandoned`) non-`queued` shipments, never `blocked`.
* When `opts.DryRun == true`, `RemoveFromShipment` and `MoveShipmentItem` use a dedicated read-only preview path that refuses if any unfinished recovery journal exists (without running recovery) and reads shipment/member artifacts via `readArtifactFromDiskReadOnly` without calling `lockShipmentLifecycleGlobal`, `GetShipment`, or `loadArtifact` (verified by pending-journal refusal and SQLite cache-miss zero-write tests).
* `validateShipmentLifecycleJournalRecord` / `loadShipmentOperationJournals` in `internal/core/shipment_ops.go` accepts both `Operation == "remove_item"` and `Operation == "move_item"`, and `reconcileShipmentLifecycleIntent` in `internal/core/shipment_recovery.go` recovers both `remove_item` and `move_item` journals through the `lockShipmentLifecycleGlobal` barrier by restoring `Preimage.Shipment` (and `Preimage.Related` for `move_item`) and `Preimage.Members` (including `scheduler_baseline_claim`) in Markdown and SQLite. `MoveShipmentItem` persists a `move_item` journal with `correlation_id`, both shipment preimages (`Preimage.Shipment` and `Preimage.Related`), AND the moved member artifact's full preimage (`Preimage.Members`, including its original `custom_fields.scheduler_baseline_claim`) plus `phase: "intent"` events before mutating the member or either manifest; skips in-memory compensation on `ErrWriteIndeterminate` so the next real `lockShipmentLifecycleGlobal` lifecycle barrier recovers both shipment manifests AND the moved member's full preimage (including `scheduler_baseline_claim`) in Markdown and SQLite; and if the second `phase: "committed"` `shipment_item_moved` append fails with `ErrWriteNotApplied`, rolls back the moved member's full preimage (including `scheduler_baseline_claim`) and both manifests in Markdown and SQLite and appends a compensating `phase: "aborted"` event carrying the same `correlation_id` to the first shipment's log (verified by real `lockShipmentLifecycleGlobal` barrier recovery of on-disk `remove_item` and `move_item` journals, claimed-member forced-remove and forced-move failure/recovery restoring `scheduler_baseline_claim`, indeterminate-write journal retention, and failure-after-first-append rollback/aborted-event tests).

Implement `ShipmentManifestOptions`, `RemoveFromShipmentResult`, `MoveShipmentItemResult`, `RemoveFromShipment`, `MoveShipmentItem`, and `remove_item` + `move_item` journal validation in `internal/core/shipment_ops.go` (and `remove_item` + `move_item` lifecycle-barrier recovery in `internal/core/shipment_recovery.go`) with unit tests in `internal/core/shipment_manifest_test.go`.

* Enforce `queued`-only default and blocked-manifest seal: unconditionally refuse `blocked` shipments (`status == "blocked"` or carrying a blocked-manifest seal) with `ErrShipmentBlockedManifestSealed` / `ErrInvalidStatus` / `blerrors.ErrShipmentConflict` even when `opts.Force == true` (in both live and `DryRun` modes), requiring governed `UnblockShipment` first; for other non-`queued` shipments (`active`, `shipped`, `abandoned`), refuse with `blerrors.ErrShipmentConflict` unless `opts.Force == true`, and require non-empty `opts.Reason` (`blerrors.ErrValidation`) whenever `opts.Force == true`.
* Support status-preserving manifest removal in `RemoveFromShipment` under a durable `remove_item` recovery journal (`Operation: "remove_item"`, `RecoveryPolicy: "rollback"`, `ShipmentID: shipmentID`, `Target: itemID`, `Preimage.Shipment` + `Preimage.Members` containing the removed item's full preimage including `custom_fields.scheduler_baseline_claim`): the item remains in `.backlogit/queue/` unassigned from the shipment manifest with its current `status` preserved (`queued` for queued shipments, `active` for claimed active shipments) and any matching `custom_fields.scheduler_baseline_claim == shipmentID` cleared on the member. On `ErrWriteIndeterminate` during `RemoveFromShipment` (whether on the member write, shipment manifest write, or audit append), skip in-memory compensation and retain the `remove_item` journal on disk for `lockShipmentLifecycleGlobal` barrier recovery; on `ErrWriteNotApplied`, roll back in-memory from the preimages and remove the journal. Add crash-between-writes (`remove_item` journal on disk recovered by `lockShipmentLifecycleGlobal`) and indeterminate-write journal-retention recovery tests for `RemoveFromShipment` in `TestU1_RemoveFromShipment_QueuedAndForceGuards`.
* Support read-only zero-write preview when `opts.DryRun == true`: do NOT call `lockShipmentLifecycleGlobal`, `GetShipment`, or `loadArtifact`; instead check the shipment lifecycle recovery journal directory read-only (refusing with a pending-recovery error if any unfinished journal exists) and read Markdown files directly via `readArtifactFromDiskReadOnly` with zero SQLite, lock-file, or JSONL writes.
* Update `validateShipmentLifecycleJournalRecord` / `loadShipmentOperationJournals` in `internal/core/shipment_ops.go` to accept both `Operation == "remove_item"` (`RecoveryPolicy == "rollback"`, `ShipmentID == shipmentID`, `Target == itemID`, `Preimage.Shipment` = shipment preimage, `Preimage.Members` = member preimages including the removed item's full preimage with its original `scheduler_baseline_claim`) and `Operation == "move_item"` (`RecoveryPolicy == "rollback"`, `ShipmentID == source_shipment_id`, `Target == target_shipment_id`, `Preimage.Shipment` = source shipment preimage, `Preimage.Members` = source member preimages including the moved member's full preimage with its original `scheduler_baseline_claim`, and `Preimage.Related` = target shipment preimage), and update `reconcileShipmentLifecycleIntent` in `internal/core/shipment_recovery.go` to recover both `remove_item` and `move_item` journals through the `lockShipmentLifecycleGlobal` barrier by restoring `Preimage.Shipment` (and `Preimage.Related` for `move_item`) and `Preimage.Members` (including `scheduler_baseline_claim`) in Markdown and SQLite.
* In `MoveShipmentItem`, acquire `lockShipmentLifecycleGlobal` and then acquire `lockShipmentMembership` on `fromShipmentID` and `toShipmentID` in canonical lexicographical order, and execute the durable correlated `move_item` recovery protocol (`intent` journal + shipment and member preimages -> member and manifest updates -> correlated `committed` `shipment_item_moved` events, retaining the journal on `ErrWriteIndeterminate` so the real `lockShipmentLifecycleGlobal` barrier in `reconcileShipmentLifecycleIntent` restores both manifests AND the moved member's full preimage including `scheduler_baseline_claim`, and rolling back the member preimage and both manifests plus appending a correlated `phase: "aborted"` event if the second audit append fails with `ErrWriteNotApplied`).
* Emit and index `shipment_item_removed` and `shipment_item_moved` events when `!opts.DryRun`.
* Files modified: `internal/core/shipment_ops.go`, `internal/core/shipment_recovery.go`, `internal/core/shipment_manifest_test.go`.

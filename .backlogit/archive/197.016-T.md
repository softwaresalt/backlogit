**Amendment A1 (Orchestrator decision under P-017, 2026-10-10)** - appended; the deliverable, file, AC, and dependencies above are unchanged.

* The prose "Harness: exempt" above is withdrawn as superseded by P-002.1: the plan's Constitution Check "justified deviation" is no longer admissible, and U6 (197.007-T) is itself exempt so it cannot be U15's harness owner. U15 carries no exemption label and no contract block.
* U15 is harness-required. Ship's harness-architect scaffolds its RED harness at the start of wave 2 (Step 2), after 197.007-T is `done` and its verdict, ns/op, profile, and target are recorded in the U6 task notes.
* RED constraints: the harness must fail on main for the right reason and pass only after the refactor, with its failing condition derived from U6's recorded verdict and target. It must be task-scoped and satisfy the six P-002.6 requirements. Shape is the harness-architect's choice. The three shapes here are examples, not a closed list: (a) a work-count characterization test of per-member directory walks and event-log reads per gate pass, (b) a U6-derived performance budget with a margin wide enough to be stable on CI, or (c) a `go/parser`/`go/ast` source-shape assertion that per-member artifact resolution and log reads are not performed inside the member loop.
* Seam and determinism limits: a work-count RED may observe only through `_test.go`-only seams (a counting wrapper, fake, or hook defined in test files). It must add no production seam and no production stub, because P-002.1 (cycle 31) forbids a production declaration ahead of its harness. A timing or performance budget may only corroborate a RED and must never be the sole RED condition, because timing is nondeterministic.
* Characterization guard (required with the RED harness): every existing shipment gate test, and every verdict, error classification, and lock order, stay unchanged.
* HALT: a REFUTED U6 verdict, a missed target, or no way to form a valid RED harness means halt and return to Stage. Do not exempt U15 and do not scaffold a substitute harness.

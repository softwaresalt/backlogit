# CAP-3 (76553D8D): Optimize ShipShipment per-member snapshot, lookup, ancestry, and ReadAllEvents buffer cost

## Problem Frame

<!-- BEGIN:problem-frame -->
`ShipShipment` (`internal/core/shipment_lifecycle.go`, `internal/core/shipment_gate.go`, `internal/events/reader.go`) suffers high per-member latency on multi-member shipments due to (1) eager 1 MiB buffer allocation/zeroing on every `events.ReadAllEvents` call, (2) two full `filepath.WalkDir` + YAML-parse scans per member in `snapshotShipArtifacts` (`findArtifact` followed by `FindArtifactPath`) and O(N) directory walks in `findArtifact`/`FindArtifactPath` even when `{id}.md` sits at the canonical path, and (3) repeated `git merge-base --is-ancestor` subprocess spawns for identical `(memberHead, shipmentHead)` pairs across members and across the two gate passes.
<!-- END:problem-frame -->

## Options

<!-- BEGIN:options -->
Option A: Remove the second `gateShipmentCompletion` pass in `ShipShipment`. Pros: halves gate calls. Cons: explicitly forbidden by `76553D8D` and `shipment_lifecycle.go:624-626` because Pass 2 re-validates after artifact locks (`B`) and item-log locks (`C`) are held.
Option B (Recommended): Keep both gate passes, lock order, and Markdown source-of-truth reloads intact, and eliminate the three concrete code-verified bottlenecks: (1) `ReadAllEvents` 1 MiB initial buffer -> 64 KiB initial / 1 MiB max (`internal/events/reader.go`), (2) `findArtifactWithPath` + direct `{id}.md` contained lookup in `findArtifact`/`FindArtifactPath`/`snapshotShipArtifacts` (`internal/core/artifacts.go`, `internal/core/shipment_lifecycle.go`), and (3) immutable `(ancestorSHA, descendantSHA)` commit-ancestry memoization in `isAncestor` (`internal/core/shipment_gate.go`). Pros: eliminates O(M * N) directory walks, 93% of `ReadAllEvents` buffer zeroing, and duplicate `git merge-base --is-ancestor` spawns across Pass 1 & Pass 2 without altering any verdict or lock invariant. Cons: touches `internal/events` and `internal/core` (decompose into width-isolated tasks).
Option C: Replace `findArtifact` with SQLite `loadArtifact` inside `attachCommitToItems`. Pros: fast DB read. Cons: violates compound learning `2026-07-28-attach-commit-repersist-must-reload-from-markdown.md` and drops `item_links` and `archived_from`/`archived_status` provenance.
<!-- END:options -->

## Chosen Direction

<!-- BEGIN:chosen-direction -->
Option B (Selected): Optimize the `ShipShipment` closure hot path across `internal/events/reader.go`, `internal/core/artifacts.go`, `internal/core/shipment_lifecycle.go`, and `internal/core/shipment_gate.go` while preserving both gate passes, verdict timing, error classification, and lock order:
1. In `internal/events/reader.go` (`ReadAllEvents`): replace the eager 1 MiB allocation `scanner.Buffer(make([]byte, 1<<20), 1<<20)` with a 64 KiB initial buffer that grows on demand up to 1 MiB (`scanner.Buffer(make([]byte, 64*1024), 1<<20)`), cutting per-call allocation and `memclrNoHeapPointers` overhead by 16x while keeping the 1 MiB max line limit.
2. In `internal/core/artifacts.go` & `internal/core/shipment_lifecycle.go`: add `findArtifactWithPath(ctx, ws, id) (*models.Artifact, string, error)` and fast direct `{id}.md` candidate resolution (with full `ensureArtifactLookupContained` + `parseFile` verification before falling back to `filepath.WalkDir`), and use `findArtifactWithPath` in `snapshotShipArtifacts` to eliminate the duplicate per-member `findArtifact` + `FindArtifactPath` directory walks (cutting O(M * N) filesystem walks to O(M) direct reads while preserving full Markdown reloads in `attachCommitToItems` per compound rule 2026-07-28).
3. In `internal/core/shipment_gate.go` (`isAncestor` / `validateMemberGateEvidence`): memoize verified immutable `(ancestorSHA, descendantSHA)` Git commit ancestry checks within the workspace/context so repeated `(memberHead, shipmentHead)` pairs across members and between Gate Pass 1 and Gate Pass 2 avoid spawning redundant `git merge-base --is-ancestor` subprocesses.
<!-- END:chosen-direction -->

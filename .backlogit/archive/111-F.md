title: Preserve archive provenance through the artifact update round-trip
updated_at: 2026-07-18T06:25:55.5443039Z
---

backlogit update on an already-archived item drops archive-only frontmatter keys (archived_from, archived_status) because models.Artifact has no typed fields for them and WriteArtifactFile re-emits only enumerated keys, making the archive record non-invertible. Add typed ArchivedFrom/ArchivedStatus fields with parse+emit, and clear them when an update transitions an item out of archived status.

Review finding CR-001 (P2): `cmd/gen-docs/main.go` sets `DisableAutoGenTag` only 2 levels deep (root and direct subcommands) via explicit loops. Works today but fragile for future 3-level commands.

**Root cause**: `generateDocs()` iterates `root.Commands()` and their `.Commands()` but does not recurse.

**Recommended fix**: Replace the two nested loops with a recursive `cobra.Command.Traverse` walk (or a simple depth-first helper) that sets `DisableAutoGenTag = true` on every command node.

**Affected file**: `cmd/gen-docs/main.go`

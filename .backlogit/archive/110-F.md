Model A extension contract: establish an owner-scoped extension namespace under the OPEN docline map (pattern docline.<owner>.*), with backlogit as the first owner (docline.backlogit.*), overloading the docline base-frontmatter v1 contract WITHOUT touching the closed top level (additionalProperties:false stays). A layered ext JSON schema (allOf composed on base v1) validates ONLY the docline.backlogit subtree, owned and versioned by backlogit. This is the schema-valid, round-trip-durable placement FOR DOCLINE DOCUMENTS (docline FromMap/ToMap preserve the whole docline map) and lets consumers such as engram be both docline- and backlogit-schema-aware. Durability of docline.backlogit ON .backlogit ARTIFACT frontmatter is a SEPARATE, still-open question: backlogit generic artifact codec (models.ArtifactFromFrontmatter / core.WriteArtifactFile) carries only custom_fields and has no docline carrier, so a top-level docline map is dropped on artifacts until an artifact-codec bridge is selected by the 109.x spike (see the reconciliation section below). Resolves the ownership fork (deliberation 052-DL); supersedes Model B tasks 107.009/010/011/012-T. Deliverables split into 3 tasks: decision doc, docline.backlogit.* convention, layered ext schema scaffold.

DURABILITY SCOPE CORRECTION (Model A, 2026-07-16). Model A resolves PLACEMENT and SCHEMA OWNERSHIP: backlogit-owned properties nest under the docline map as docline.backlogit.* and the docline base top level stays additionalProperties:false. Round-trip durability applies to docline DOCUMENTS (the engram/graphtor corpus), where docline own codec preserves the whole docline map (internal/docline BaseFrontmatter.Docline, FromMap/ToMap). It does NOT automatically apply to .backlogit ARTIFACT frontmatter: backlogit generic artifact codec captures only custom_fields (models.ArtifactFromFrontmatter) and re-emits only struct fields plus custom_fields (core.WriteArtifactFile); models.Artifact has no docline carrier, so a top-level docline map is DROPPED on artifacts. The source-stash grounding note (in custom_fields) that the backlogit artifact codec preserves nested maps is TOO BROAD for artifacts. This feature deliberately keeps the artifact-codec bridge UNRESOLVED: the bridge selection (add a Docline carrier to models.Artifact, or another actually-preserved location) is owned by the 109.x spike (109.004-T) and must be proven by an executable round-trip test before size is persisted under docline.backlogit on .backlogit artifacts.

# CAP-3 (63909DAE): Harden TestU172_LockOrder and TestArchiveItemGovernance lock-contention synchronization under full-suite load

## Problem Frame

<!-- BEGIN:problem-frame -->
Stash `63909DAE` (from `197-S` post-merge closure, PR #491): under concurrent full-suite load, `TestU172_LockOrder_ArchiveItemDoesNotHoldArtifactLockWhileWaitingForItemLog` (`internal/core/artifact_mutation_lock_order_test.go`) and `TestArchiveItemGovernance_WaitsForGlobalLifecycleLock` (`internal/core/shipment_archive_governance_test.go`) fail intermittently while passing in isolation because (1) `ArchiveItem` only waits `defaultGateLockBoundedWait` (`3s`) for contended locks and performs `recoverPendingShipmentOperations` + `archiveRelatedShipmentIDs` between acquiring `global` and reaching `C`, and (2) `runU172CBeforeBWriter` and post-unlock waits use tight `3s`/`5s` deadlines that expire under heavy disk/CPU load.
<!-- END:problem-frame -->

## Options

<!-- BEGIN:options -->
Option A: Increase `defaultGateLockBoundedWait` globally from `3s` to `60s`. Pros: simple. Cons: slows down every deliberate busy-lock rejection test (`ErrTaskBusy`) across the entire test suite by 20x!
Option B (Recommended): Synchronize `TestU172_LockOrder_ArchiveItemDoesNotHoldArtifactLockWhileWaitingForItemLog` and `TestArchiveItemGovernance_WaitsForGlobalLifecycleLock` / `TestArchiveItemGovernance_WaitsForShipmentMembershipLock` on deterministic contention hooks before starting their negative hold windows, shorten the post-contention hold to `50ms` (well within `defaultGateLockBoundedWait = 3s`), and use `u172ObservationWait` (`30s`) for `runU172CBeforeBWriter` and post-unlock completion waits. Pros: keeps `defaultGateLockBoundedWait = 3s` fast for negative tests while making positive lock-order and governance-wait tests immune to CPU/disk scheduling delays under concurrent full-suite load. Cons: none.
Option C: Skip lock-order timing assertions when CPU load is high. Pros: no failures. Cons: violates P-004.
<!-- END:options -->

## Chosen Direction

<!-- BEGIN:chosen-direction -->
Option B (Selected): Harden `TestU172_LockOrder_ArchiveItemDoesNotHoldArtifactLockWhileWaitingForItemLog` (`internal/core/artifact_mutation_lock_order_test.go`) and `TestArchiveItemGovernance_WaitsForGlobalLifecycleLock` / `TestArchiveItemGovernance_WaitsForShipmentMembershipLock` (`internal/core/shipment_archive_governance_test.go`):
1. In `TestU172_LockOrder_ArchiveItemDoesNotHoldArtifactLockWhileWaitingForItemLog`: add a deterministic pre-item-log-wait notification hook (or synchronize on `ArchiveItem` reaching `lockItemLogsSorted` after `recoverPendingShipmentOperations` and `archiveRelatedShipmentIDs` complete) before running the 250ms negative probe on `B` and `runU172CBeforeBWriter`, and pass `u172ObservationWait` (`30 * time.Second`) instead of `defaultGateLockBoundedWait` (`3 * time.Second`) to `runU172CBeforeBWriter` so heavy CPU/disk contention under full-suite load never causes premature writer deadline expiry or releases `C` before `ArchiveItem` reaches `lockItemLogsSorted`.
2. In `TestArchiveItemGovernance_WaitsForGlobalLifecycleLock` and `TestArchiveItemGovernance_WaitsForShipmentMembershipLock`: synchronize on deterministic lock-attempt notifications before starting the negative-window check, keep the negative hold window short (`50ms` after contention is observed) so `ArchiveItem`'s 3-second `defaultGateLockBoundedWait` cannot expire while the test goroutine is descheduled under load, and increase the post-unlock completion wait from `defaultGateLockBoundedWait + 2*time.Second` (`5s`) to `u172ObservationWait` (`30s`).
<!-- END:chosen-direction -->

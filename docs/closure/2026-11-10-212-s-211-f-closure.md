## Rollback trigger and procedure

Trigger: any response crossover, duplicate owner, root mismatch, lost
update, or unexpected peer termination; alternatively, two consecutive
startup/IPC errors or a read exceeding the proposed 10-second threshold.

Proposed operator-controlled mitigation:

1. Stop further adoption and mutations; preserve bounded diagnostics.
2. Verify affected workspace, exact PID/start identity, executable, and
   active-client ownership. Do not stop unrelated processes.
3. Obtain explicit approval before shutdown/replacement or filesystem
   restoration. Never delete locks or reset the store as an automatic fix.
4. Route clients to the last known-good compatible binary only after
   confirming its availability and storage compatibility.
5. Recheck roots and read behavior; reconcile any suspected durable-state
   discrepancy before resuming writes.
